Ethics & corrections

Dank Recovery News follows the Society of Professional Journalists Code of Ethics.

Accuracy

We verify information before we publish it, tell readers where it came from, and link to original sources when we can.

Fairness

When a story makes a claim about a person or organization, we give them a reasonable chance to respond.

Privacy

We don’t identify private individuals as being in recovery, or as members of any recovery fellowship, without their permission.

Opinion and advertising

Opinion pieces are labeled as opinion. Sponsored content is labeled as sponsored.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it and add a note to the story saying what changed. To report an error, email timothy@dankrecovery.com with the subject “Correction.”
