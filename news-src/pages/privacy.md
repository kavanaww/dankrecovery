Short version: we collect your email address if you give it to us, and that's it.

## What we collect

If you sign up for the newsletter, we keep the email address you enter. We don't ask for your name or anything else, and reading the site doesn't require an account.

## What we do with it

We use your email address to send you Dank Recovery News and other Dank Recovery emails. We don't sell it, rent it or share it with advertisers.

## Getting off the list

Email [timothy@dankrecovery.com](mailto:timothy@dankrecovery.com?subject=Unsubscribe) with the subject "Unsubscribe" and we'll remove you.

## Other services this site uses

This site doesn't run ad trackers or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and the signup form is handled by Google. Like any web host, those services receive your IP address when your browser loads the page.
