Dank Recovery News covers recovery news and trends, and what's happening in the fellowship worldwide: AA, NA and other fellowships, the treatment industry, policy, research, overdose data and sober culture.

## Who runs it

Dank Recovery News is published by Dank Recovery LLC, based in St. Charles, Missouri. Dank Recovery has been around since 2015. Sponsored content is always labeled, and nobody pays for news coverage.

## Timothy Kavanagh, editor and reporter {#timothy-kavanagh}

Timothy Kavanagh owns Dank Recovery LLC and reports and edits Dank Recovery News. He has 14 years sober and has worked in addiction recovery since 2013.

Email: [timothy@dankrecovery.com](mailto:timothy@dankrecovery.com)

## Disclosure

Timothy also works in the addiction treatment field. When a story involves his own work or an organization he works with, the story says so, or we don't cover it.

## Standards

We follow the Society of Professional Journalists Code of Ethics. Read our [ethics and corrections policy](/news/ethics/).
